Privacy Notice

Last updated: {{ current_year }}

Student OS is a school management tool built by a solo developer, currently running as a demo and pilot with a small number of schools. This notice explains, in plain language, what data the platform handles and how it's protected. It isn't a substitute for a full legal privacy policy — if your school is considering using this beyond a pilot, ask for that conversation directly.

What data is collected

Depending on your role, the platform stores: your name, username, and email; for students — attendance records, grades, assignment submissions, and (if provided) parent contact details; for teachers — department and course assignments; and messages sent through the built-in messaging feature.

Why it's collected

Solely to run the core features you're using it for — attendance tracking, grading, assignments, and communication between students, teachers, and admins within your school. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Who can see it

Access is role-based and scoped to your school. Students see their own records; teachers see records for their courses; admins see records for their school. Data from one school is not visible to another school on the platform.

How it's protected

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Uploaded files (assignments, submissions, documents) require login to access. Email addresses linked to an account go through a verification step before being used for password recovery.

Pilot-stage limitations, honestly stated

This is an early-stage project. It has not been through a formal third-party security audit, and it does not yet have automated backup guarantees beyond what the hosting provider offers by default. If your school is piloting this, please avoid relying on it as the sole record of critical data until those guarantees are in place.

Questions or concerns

If you'd like data corrected or removed, or have any concern about how information is being handled, reach out via the contact form and it'll be addressed directly.
